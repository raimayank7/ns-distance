About the trip

  • The road distance from Mumbai (Maharashtra) to Gangtok (Sikkim) is 2,270 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 4,540 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 1,849 km. The road is 23% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 27 h 04 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 27 to 37 hours.
  • Gangtok is to the north-east of Mumbai; coming back you travel south-west.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Jabalpur.
