About the trip

  • The road distance from Ranchi (Jharkhand) to Rishikesh (Uttarakhand) is 1,247 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 2,494 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 1,025 km. The road is 22% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 14 h 57 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 15 to 20 hours.
  • Rishikesh is to the north-west of Ranchi; coming back you travel south-east.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Ayodhya.
