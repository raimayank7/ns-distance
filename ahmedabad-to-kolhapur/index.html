About the trip

  • The road distance from Ahmedabad (Gujarat) to Kolhapur (Maharashtra) is 865 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 1,730 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 725 km. The road is 19% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 10 h 57 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 11 to 15 hours.
  • Kolhapur is to the south of Ahmedabad; coming back you travel north.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Nashik.
