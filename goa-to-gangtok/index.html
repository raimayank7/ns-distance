About the trip

  • The road distance from Goa to Gangtok (Sikkim) is 2,518 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 5,036 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 2,015 km. The road is 25% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 30 h 31 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 31 to 41 hours.
  • Gangtok is to the north-east of Goa; coming back you travel south-west.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Bhilai.
