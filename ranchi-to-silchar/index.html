About the trip

  • The road distance from Ranchi (Jharkhand) to Silchar (Assam) is 1,398 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 2,796 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 778 km. The road is 80% longer than the straight line, which is usual for hill roads.
  • The map's driving time is 17 h 19 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 17 to 23 hours.
  • Silchar is to the east of Ranchi; coming back you travel west.
