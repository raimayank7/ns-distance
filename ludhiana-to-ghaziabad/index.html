About the trip

  • The road distance from Ludhiana (Punjab) to Ghaziabad (Uttar Pradesh) is 337 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 674 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 293 km. The road is 15% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 4 h 04 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 4 to 5.5 hours.
  • Ghaziabad is to the south-east of Ludhiana; coming back you travel north-west.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Karnal.
