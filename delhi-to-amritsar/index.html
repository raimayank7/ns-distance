About the trip

  • The road distance from Delhi to Amritsar (Punjab) is 464 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 928 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 401 km. The road is 16% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 5 h 35 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 5.5 to 7.5 hours.
  • Amritsar is to the north-west of Delhi; coming back you travel south-east.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Patiala.
