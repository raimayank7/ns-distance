About the trip

  • The road distance from Ranchi (Jharkhand) to Srinagar (Jammu and Kashmir) is 1,934 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 3,868 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 1,571 km. The road is 23% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 23 h 55 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 24 to 32 hours.
  • Srinagar is to the north-west of Ranchi; coming back you travel south-east.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Bareilly.
