About the trip

  • The road distance from Pune (Maharashtra) to Indore (Madhya Pradesh) is 605 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 1,210 km.

  • The map's driving time is 7 h 30 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 7.5 to 10 hours.
