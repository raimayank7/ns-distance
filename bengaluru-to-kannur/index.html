About the trip

  • The road distance from Bengaluru (Karnataka) to Kannur (Kerala) is 318 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 636 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 272 km. The road is 17% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 4 h 49 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 5 to 6.5 hours.
  • Kannur is to the south-west of Bengaluru; coming back you travel north-east.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Mysuru.
