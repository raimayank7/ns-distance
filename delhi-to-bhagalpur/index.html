About the trip

  • The road distance from Delhi to Bhagalpur (Bihar) is 1,219 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 2,438 km.

  • The map's driving time is 14 h 08 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 14 to 19 hours.
  • Bhagalpur is to the east of Delhi; coming back you travel north-west.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Ayodhya.
