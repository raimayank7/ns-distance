About the trip

  • The road distance from Panipat (Haryana) to Manali (Himachal Pradesh) is 427 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 854 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 318 km. The road is 34% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 5 h 50 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 6 to 8 hours.
  • Manali is to the north of Panipat; coming back you travel south.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Chandigarh.
