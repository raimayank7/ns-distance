About the trip

  • The road distance from Goa to Manali (Himachal Pradesh) is 2,270 km. The return trip is the same road, so a round trip is about 4,540 km.
  • The straight-line distance is 1,893 km. The road is 20% longer than the straight line.
  • The map's driving time is 27 h 33 min without traffic or stops. On Indian roads, with tolls, towns and breaks, plan for 28 to 37 hours.
  • Manali is to the north of Goa; coming back you travel south.
  • The larger city nearest the middle of the straight line is Ujjain.
